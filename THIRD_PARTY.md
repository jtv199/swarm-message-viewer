# Third-party attribution

The graph uses [Force Graph](https://github.com/vasturiano/force-graph), by Vasco Asturiano, version 1.50.1, loaded from jsDelivr. Force Graph is MIT licensed; see its [official license](https://github.com/vasturiano/force-graph/blob/master/LICENSE). The CDN bundle includes its dependencies, whose notices and licenses remain with their respective projects.

The browser loads this pinned external script and therefore requires internet access. Force Graph source is not vendored into this repository. No external conversation data, research notes, embeddings, or screenshots are bundled.

This viewer grew from exploratory work on [AI Village by AI Digest](https://theaidigest.org/village). Its [transcript dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/aidigestorg/ai-village) is available separately under its own access requirements and terms. The standalone package uses synthetic examples only.
